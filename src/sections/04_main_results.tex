We now derive a formula for sums of powers.
\begin{align*}
  \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m}
  = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{n} \binom{j-t}{k} \right] \Delta^{k} t^m.
\end{align*}
Setting $r=0$ in Lemma~\eqref{lem:forward-hockey-stick-identity} gives the following closed form for ordinary sums of powers.
\begin{proposition}[Ordinary sums of powers]
  \label{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $m$ and an arbitrary integer $t$,
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \left[ \binom{n-t+1}{k+1} - \binom{1-t}{k+1} \right] \Delta^{k} t^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}
The basis $\binom{j-t+r}{k+r}$ in Lemma~\eqref{lem:forward-hockey-stick-identity} is useful because it yields closed forms for sums of powers by varying the parameter $r$. The next example illustrates the case $r=1$.

The preceding example contains binomial coefficients with negative upper indices. These coefficients are determined by the identity $\binom{-n}{k} = (-1)^{k} \binom{k+n-1}{k}$. Setting $t=0$ in Proposition~\eqref{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers} gives another well-known identity for sums of powers, discussed in~\cite[p. 190]{graham1994concrete} and in~\cite{pfaff2007deriving}.
\begin{corollary}
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $m$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m} = \sum_{j=0}^{m} \binom{n+1}{j+1} \Delta^{j} 0^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{corollary}

Thus, Stirling numbers of the second kind $\stirlingii{n}{k}$ arise naturally from forward Newton interpolation for powers. Setting $t=1$ in Proposition~\eqref{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers} gives another well-known special case.
\begin{corollary}
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $m$,
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m} = \sum_{j=0}^{m} \binom{n}{j+1} \Delta^{j} 1^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{corollary}

The work in~\cite{cereceda2022sums} gives formulas for sums of powers in terms of generalized Stirling numbers of the second kind $\stirlingii{k}{j}_{r}$. Specifically, it gives the following result.
\begin{proposition}[Cereceda (2022)]
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $k$,
  \begin{align}
    \label{eq:cereceda-sums-1}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{k} &= \sum_{j=0}^{k} j!
    \left[
      \binom{n+1-r}{j+1} + (-1)^j \binom{r+j-1}{j+1}
    \right] \stirlingii{k}{j}_{r}.
  \end{align}
  The same work also gives
  \begin{align}
    \label{eq:cereceda-sums-2}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{k} &= \sum_{j=0}^{k} j!
    \left[
      \binom{n+1+r}{j+1} + \binom{r+1}{j+1}
    \right] \stirlingii{k}{j}_{-r}.
  \end{align}
\end{proposition}
Equation~\eqref{eq:cereceda-sums-1} is a special case of Proposition~\eqref{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers}, obtained by setting $t=r$ and applying the upper-binomial negation identity in Lemma~\eqref{lem:upper-binomial-negation} to $\binom{1-t}{k+1}$. The forward finite differences of powers can also be expressed in terms of generalized Stirling numbers of the second kind:
\begin{align}
  \label{eq:gen-stirling-finit-difference}
  \Delta^{k} t^m = k! \stirlingii{m}{k}_{t}.
\end{align}
This identity shows that generalized Stirling numbers of the second kind $\stirlingii{n}{k}_t$ arise naturally from forward Newton interpolation for powers evaluated at an arbitrary integer $t$. Equation~\eqref{eq:cereceda-sums-2} is another special case of Proposition~\eqref{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers}, obtained by setting $t=-r$ and using Equation~\eqref{eq:gen-stirling-finit-difference}.

We now consider double sums of powers. Setting $r=1$ in the hockey-stick identity from Lemma~\eqref{lem:forward-hockey-stick-identity} and including the correction terms $\KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{0}$ gives the following formula.
\begin{proposition}[Double sums of powers]
  \label{prop:double-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $m$ and an arbitrary integer $t$,
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{2}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+2}{k+2}
      - \binom{1-t}{k+1} \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{0}
      - \binom{2-t}{k+2} \KnuthRFoldSum{0}{n}{0}
    \right] \Delta^{k} t^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}
The zero-power sums satisfy $\KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{0}=n$ and $\KnuthRFoldSum{0}{n}{0}=1$.
Applying the upper-binomial negation identity from Lemma~\eqref{lem:upper-binomial-negation} gives the following equivalent formula.
\begin{proposition}[Double sums of powers negated]
  \label{prop:double-sums-of-powers-negated}
  For non-negative integers $n$ and $m$ and an arbitrary integer $t$,
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{2}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+2}{k+2} + (-1)^k \binom{k+t-1}{k+1} n
      + (-1)^{k+1} \binom{k+t-1}{k+2}
    \right] \Delta^{j} t^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}

Repeating the procedure used for $\KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m}$ and $\KnuthRFoldSum{2}{n}{m}$ gives a general formula for $r$-fold sums of powers.
\begin{theorem}[Multifold sums of powers]
  \label{thm:multifold-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $r$, $n$, and $m$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+r}{k+r}
      -
      \sum_{s=0}^{r-1}
      \binom{r-s-t}{k+r-s}
      \KnuthRFoldSum{s}{n}{0}
    \right] \Delta^{k} t^{m}.
  \end{align*}
\end{theorem}
Using Lemma~\eqref{lem:multifold-sum-of-zero-powers}, we obtain the following pure binomial form.
\begin{proposition}[Multifold binomial sums of powers]
  \label{prop:multifold-binomial-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $r$, $n$, and $m$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+r}{k+r}
      -
      \sum_{s=0}^{r-1}
      \binom{r-s-t}{k+r-s}
      \binom{s+n-1}{s}
    \right] \Delta^{k} t^{m}.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}
Explicitly,
\begin{align*}
  \KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{m}
  = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
  \bigg[
    \tbinom{n-t+r}{k+r}
    -
    \tbinom{r-t}{k+r}
    \tbinom{n-1}{0}
    -
    \tbinom{r-1-t}{k+r-1}
    \tbinom{n}{1}
    -
    \cdots
    -
    \tbinom{2-t}{k+2}
    \tbinom{r+n-3}{r-2}
    -
    \tbinom{1-t}{k+1}
    \tbinom{r+n-2}{r-1}
  \bigg] \Delta^{k} t^{m}.
\end{align*}
